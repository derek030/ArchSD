ERUDITE

Government Primary School

First Steel MiC 30-classroom Primary School cum Kindergarten in Hong Kong, combining construction innovation, efficiency and sustainability.

Category
Sustainable Construction
Sustainable Design Approaches
Location
8 On Hei Street, Sai Kung District, NT

Erudite Government Primary School is a primary school development targeting BEAM Plus Platinum certification, establishing a bold rethink of low-carbon, smart construction and sustainable educational infrastructure. Grounded in human-centric design, the project transitions traditional school layouts into an ultra-low-carbon, nature-integrated learning landscape.

To maximize construction safety, quality control, and carbon reduction, the project extensively deployed Modular Integrated Construction (MiC) technology. Using just 125 standardized steel MiC modules anchored by in-situ concrete slabs, high off-site prefabrication improved overall spatial efficiency by 5% and cut transportation carbon emissions by 25%. The circular footprint was engineered specifically to optimize tower crane hoisting radii, streamlining assembly while minimizing on-site waste, noise, and environmental disturbance.

The campus integrates active green technologies and smart operational systems. High-efficiency roof-mounted photovoltaic (PV) arrays harvest renewable energy, smart IoT sensors optimize energy usage across facilities, and a comprehensive greywater recycling system irrigates the surrounding vegetated landscapes.

The building’s passive design leverages active-passive environmental zoning. Larger volumes like the elevated ball court act as a built acoustic shield against traffic noise, protecting quiet teaching zones. A central circular courtyard combined with podium-landscaped voids drives natural cross-ventilation and daylighting throughout, significantly reducing HVAC operational energy demand.
