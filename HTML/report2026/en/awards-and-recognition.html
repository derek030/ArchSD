Awards and Recognition

We strive for excellence in facilities development and upkeep, industry and community contributions. Awards are a strong testament to our innovative solutions for the benefit of the living environment and thriving communities.

In 2025, we won over 60 prestigious awards recognising our contributions in green buildings, creative urban design, construction and research innovation, heritage conservation and project management. Below is a selection of some key awards from local and global institutions.
